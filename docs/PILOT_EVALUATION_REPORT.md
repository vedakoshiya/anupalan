# Anupalan MSME Pilot Evaluation & UAT Report

**Pilot Period:** October 2026  
**Pilot Target Groups:**
1. **Practicing Professionals:** 4 Chartered Accountants (Surat & Ahmedabad GST Practitioners)
2. **MSME Business Owners:** 10 Micro and Small Enterprise Proprietors (Surat Weaving Units, Diamond Polishing Job Workers, Rajkot Engineering MSMEs)

---

## 1. Pilot Evaluation Methodology

Participants conducted interactive testing across 4 key operational modules:
1. **GST Job Work Rate Resolution:** Testing queries on Entry 26(i) (textiles 5% vs general 12%).
2. **Composition Scheme Thresholds:** Historical (75 Lakhs) vs current (1.5 Crore) rules.
3. **MSME Payment Timelines under Section 43B(h):** Verification of the 15/45-day disallowance rule.
4. **Gujarat MSME Capital & Interest Subsidy:** Eligibility and application timelines under the Gujarat Industrial Policy.
